Sheer Strength Amazon Growth Strategy

Prepared by FTR  ·  Amazon & Walmart Growth

Summary

Sheer Strength has ambitious growth goals, with a target of $200K in monthly revenue within six months and $400K within twelve months.

To get there, we need to focus on three main areas:

  • Customer Acquisition: Traffic is down significantly, and ALPHA and SHRED have lost visibility in Amazon search. Conversion is holding up relatively well, so bringing more relevant traffic back to the listings is a key priority.
  • Premium Positioning & Conversion: The current listings and creative don't yet reflect the premium brand Sheer Strength wants to build. We need to improve how the products are presented, communicate their value more clearly, and give customers stronger reasons to choose them.
  • Subscribe & Save & Retention: Active subscriptions are declining despite very aggressive first-order discounts. We need to review the current offers, improve retention, and better understand how much we can afford to spend to acquire a customer.

Our first priority is to bring traffic and customer acquisition back up, while working on the listing and creative improvements needed to support conversion and the brand's longer-term premium positioning.

At the same time, we'll review the Subscribe & Save setup and work toward better LTV/CAC tracking so we can make more informed decisions about advertising spend and promotions.

We'll also prepare for Black Friday/Cyber Monday and New Year, New You, with January being a particularly good opportunity for this catalog.

Growth Areas
01 | Customer Acquisition & Growth Recovery

Traffic is one of the biggest issues we need to address.

From January through September 2026, sessions were down 27% compared with the same period last year, while revenue was down only 3%.

This tells us that conversion is holding up relatively well, but we're bringing significantly fewer shoppers to the listings.

We can also see from Search Query Performance that ALPHA and SHRED have lost a significant amount of generic search visibility, both in impression share and click share.

The opportunity here is to bring that traffic back, but we need to be selective about where we invest, as profitability looks very different across the four products.

Key Recommendations
Recover search visibility for ALPHA and SHRED.
  • Identify the generic searches where we've lost the most visibility.
  • Focus PPC efforts on bringing back relevant impressions, clicks, and customers.
  • Track Search Query Performance alongside sessions, conversion, and profitability to see what's improving.
Monitor search performance and continue testing listing elements.
  • Track changes in search impression share, click share, and conversion following the initial listing optimizations.
  • Identify opportunities for further improvements to main images, titles, Item Highlights, and other key listing elements that influence clicks and conversion.
  • Run additional experiments or A/B tests where needed to understand which versions perform better.

02 | Premium Positioning & Conversion Optimization

Sheer Strength wants to move toward a more premium position in the market, with a longer-term goal of reaching higher price levels.

Right now, the Amazon listings and creative don't fully reflect that direction.

The brand already has a strong concept with Father Figure and Refuse to Fade. We need to bring more of that identity into the Amazon experience while doing a better job of showing customers what makes the products different and why they're worth the price.

Key Recommendations
Fully optimize all four core listings.
  • Complete full SEO optimization, including titles, bullets, Item Highlights, descriptions, and backend attributes.
  • Optimize for both traditional Amazon search and Amazon's AI-powered shopping experiences, including Alexa for shopping, by making product information clear, complete, and structured so Amazon can better understand the products and their relevance to customer searches and questions.
  • Redesign the image stacks and Premium A+ Content.
  • Make the key benefits and product differences easier to understand.
  • Give more attention to formulation quality, ingredients, transparency, and supporting evidence.
  • Make sure the content is easy to read and understand on mobile.

03 | Subscribe & Save & Customer Retention

Repeat customers and subscribers are valuable for Sheer Strength, but the current subscription performance is moving in the wrong direction.

Active subscriptions are down 12.9% year over year, from 2,466 to 2,147.

At the same time, the current first-order Subscribe & Save offers are extremely aggressive. When we combine the coupons with the standard subscription discount, customers are getting approximately 52–57% off their first order.

That means they're paying significantly more when their next subscription order arrives.

We don't yet know how much this affects retention, but it's something we need to look into, especially as the brand moves toward premium positioning.

Key Recommendations
Test less aggressive first-order offers.
  • Review the current coupons and Subscribe & Save discounts for each product.
  • Test smaller coupons and different discount combinations.
  • Compare how these changes affect conversion, new subscriptions, retention, and profitability.
Focus on growing and keeping subscribers.
  • Track new subscriptions, cancellations, and overall subscription growth.
  • Look at how many customers stay subscribed for their second and third orders.
  • Use clearer product education and consistent-use messaging to help customers understand the value of staying subscribed.

Phase 2 | Average Order Value & Customer Value

Once we've made progress on recovering traffic, improving conversion, and optimizing the core listings, we'll shift our focus toward additional opportunities to increase average order value (AOV) and customer value.

This will include reviewing the performance of existing Virtual Bundles, exploring multipack opportunities, and identifying ways to improve cross-selling across the catalog.

For the first 90 days, our priority is to recover growth and strengthen the existing catalog before moving into these opportunities.

Creative Strategy

As we’re seeking to position as top-of-the-market premium, we need to demonstrate visible proof of justification for the higher price point. Our main way to achieve this is through transparency - full clinical doses, zero proprietary blends, a third-party-tested, made-in-USA GMP facility, and a 90-day guarantee.

The creatives need to reflect this through overall consistent branding, targeted messaging and lifestyle imagery aimed at our specific desired audience, in a way that creates an emotional connection and allows the consumer to envision his improved life when taking Sheer Strength supplements.

Main images
  • Enhance bottle image - ideally real product photography will help here, but we can also make main-image-only label adjustments to ensure key information is conveyed to the consumer from the SERP.
  • Ensure legibility of text on label at mobile thumbnail size (mobile ready hero image)
  • Lean into feature colors for each product to help stand out
  • Trust build with any additional badges (e.g. made in USA)
USPs

We need to clearly highlight our USPs for each product within the first 3 images - all top competitors are placing supplement facts immediately after the main image, so we’ll need to ensure that we’re then making it clear to consumers why they should choose us.

  1. What it does for me (outcome-led; universal benefit first, father framing second)
  2. Why it's different: full clinical doses, zero proprietary blends. Directly answers the #1 past failed solution ("underdosed supplements that did nothing").
  3. Trust: third-party tested, made in USA, GMP, plant- and mineral-based
  4. Risk reversal: 90-day money-back guarantee (currently buried in FAQs; it should be on-image)
  5. Expectation setting: a 30 / 60 / 90 day timeline and encouragement of consistent use. This is the top negative review theme for every product ("no noticeable difference": DHEA ~55, Nitro ~11, Alpha 8). Reviews like that often come from mismatched expectations, and the timeline reframes "it didn't work in a week."

All benefit copy should be cross-checked against the client's claims-validation sheets. Only use validated claims, include the DSHEA disclaimer, and use no numbers or percentages tied to outcomes.

We want to ensure all products have the same framework for imagery, for consistency within the brand.

Product-specific objection images
  • Alpha: "No stimulants. No caffeine. No crash." (answers "will it jitter me?") Note: hair changes are mentioned in negative reviews, so the FAQ should cover them honestly.
  • DHEA: Address the "bottle is mostly empty/cotton" complaint (~5) directly with a "60 full-dose capsules, one small capsule a day" visual showing the capsule against a fingertip. Add a "is DHEA right for me?" image (who it's for, talk to your doctor), because safety concerns are a real negative theme. The honest-friend voice fits this well.
  • Nitro: The reviews say "bigger pumps and vascularity," but the brand avoids "insane pumps." Visually portray this while avoiding bro-science. Lead with Nitrosigine® 1500mg ("full clinical dose, where many brands use 500mg") as the proof point.
  • Shred: Nausea and stomach upset is the #1 negative (~9 + ~5 diarrhea). Add a "how to start" graphic: 2 capsules with food in week 1, then 3. Never on an empty stomach, none after 3pm. Pair it with "No jitters" (L-Theanine), which is a positive theme (~16).
Cross-selling

Utilize group shots across image stack and A+ to enable product discovery, use brand story and store to push cross-selling.

Specific story-telling and shopping by goal (storefront) can assist here.

Subscribe & Save

S&S matters strategically (DHEA's profit engine, the client's goal to grow subscriptions monthly, and the request for an NTB S&S-led target ACOS). The brand belief "Consistency beats intensity. Staying on the wagon is the whole game." makes S&S part of the product promise, not a discount tactic:

  • Listing image: "Results build over 60–90 days. Subscribe so you never miss a day." Pair it with the timeline image.
  • A+ module: "Built for consistency" (subscribe, save, skip or cancel anytime).
  • Store: a dedicated S&S tile or section: "Set it. Show up daily."
New Year creative

Shred peaks in Q4 into Q1, and January is when this customer finally acts. We’ll develop specific creatives for this period - e.g. storefront banner/brand story tile.

Advertising Strategy

We'll focus on three things:

  • Cut wasted spend: Remove the searches and campaigns that are spending without generating sales.
  • Recover search visibility: Invest in the main searches where ALPHA and SHRED have lost ground.
  • Set targets based on customer value: Base our advertising targets on what a customer is worth over time, not just their first order.
Key Recommendations
Recover search visibility for ALPHA and SHRED.
  • Focus spend on three to five main searches per product, such as "testosterone booster for men" and "fat burner for men."
  • Increase investment on these searches as the new listings go live, so the extra traffic lands on stronger product pages.
  • Track organic rank and Search Query Performance weekly to confirm the spend is working.
Reduce wasted spend.
  • Pause or reduce searches and campaigns that are spending without converting.
  • Fix overlapping campaigns, where the same keyword is running in more than one campaign and pushing up our own costs.
  • Simplify the account structure, which currently includes more than 1,600 campaigns, many from previous management.
Close gaps in campaign coverage.
  • Protect branded searches across all four products with Sponsored Brands, especially as off-Amazon marketing restarts.
  • Rebuild competitor targeting, starting with ALPHA, where it is currently almost inactive.
  • Use Sponsored Brands video to bring the Father Figure / Refuse to Fade story into search results.
  • Add retargeting to bring back shoppers who viewed the products but didn't buy.

Amazon Events
Black Friday / Cyber Monday

Focus: Capture seasonal demand without relying on overly aggressive discounts.

Key Recommendations
  • Confirm which products will participate and what offers are available.
  • Review discounts against product profitability and existing Subscribe & Save incentives.
  • Avoid stacking offers that make the discounts unnecessarily deep.
  • Align PPC budgets and priorities with the final event offers.
  • Review sales, profitability, and new subscription acquisition after the event.

With BFCM approaching, the priority is to finalize the offers and advertising plan rather than introduce major changes that won't be ready in time.

New Year, New You

Focus: Leverage increased interest in fitness, strength, and wellness products as customers shop for their New Year's resolutions.

Key Recommendations
  • Capture increased seasonal demand: Use the expected rise in interest around fitness, strength, and wellness to bring more relevant traffic to Sheer Strength's products.
  • Introduce New Year, New You messaging: Incorporate relevant New Year's resolution messaging across advertising and creative assets, connecting the products to customers' fitness and wellness goals while staying consistent with the brand's premium positioning.
  • Consider a seasonal promotion: Review December and early January performance, including the impact of our listing and advertising optimizations, to determine whether an additional offer would help capture more demand.
  • Adjust advertising based on performance: Look for opportunities to increase investment in products and campaigns that are performing well during the seasonal demand increase.
